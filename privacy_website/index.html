📖 Introduction & Privacy Guarantee

Welcome to "Quran & Dhikr" ("Application"). We respect your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting your personal information.

Privacy Commitment: "Quran & Dhikr" is a completely free, ad-free Islamic application. We do NOT collect, track, sell, or share any personal identification data or user information with third parties.

🔒 Permissions & Data Usage

  • Location Access (Optional):

    The application requests location access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) solely to calculate accurate Prayer Times and Qibla Direction (compass angle towards Mecca). Your location data is processed locally on your phone and is never transmitted to external servers.

  • Local Storage (On-Device Database):

    All your reading preferences, bookmarks, tasbih counter progress, memorization plans, and notes are saved strictly on your local device storage using an encrypted Room database.

  • Network Access:

    Internet connectivity is used exclusively to stream Quranic audio recitations from trusted public Islamic servers (e.g. MP3Quran) and fetch prayer calculation data.

  • Notifications & Haptics:

    Used for Prayer Time alerts and haptic vibration feedback during digital Tasbih counting.

  • Child Safety Point of Contact: For any child safety or CSAE concerns regarding Quran & Dhikr, contact our designated Child Safety Lead at support@official-web.online.
